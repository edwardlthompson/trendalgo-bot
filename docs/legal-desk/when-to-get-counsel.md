# When to get real counsel (Tier B)

> **Not legal advice.** TrendAlgo’s Legal Research Desk does not replace a licensed attorney.

## Triggers (stop and seek counsel / clinic)

- First **non-owner** charge or Alpha Share / performance-fee enrollment
- Advertising that others must pay a performance fee to use official builds
- Raising capital, forming investor agreements, or selling equity
- Ambiguity whether your settlement flow looks like money transmission in PR/US
- Multi-tenant or hosted trading keys (already rejected in ADR-0008 — do not ship)

## Free / low-cost starting points (no endorsement)

| Resource | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| [SCORE](https://www.score.org/) | Mentors; may refer legal clinics |
| University law-school clinics | Search “startup clinic” + your jurisdiction |
| State / PR bar lawyer referral | Paid consult; ask for flat fee for software-vendor review |
| [Legal Services Corporation find help](https://www.lsc.gov/about-lsc/what-legal-aid/find-legal-aid) | Eligibility varies |

## Repo path after counsel

Drop a short note under `docs/legal-desk/counsel/` (date + attorney/clinic name + scope reviewed). Then H-006b may be approved with a note that references that file — never invent “attorney approved” without it.
